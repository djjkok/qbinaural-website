FAQ

A little moreclarity.

Do I need headphones?

Yes, use stereo headphones so each ear receives its own tone for the intended binaural effect.

Does it work with the screen locked?

Yes. Background playback is supported, subject to standard iOS audio behavior.

Can I use my own audio?

My Music allows you to use audio selected from your device, processed locally.

Is QBINAURAL medical treatment?

No. QBINAURAL is a wellness audio app and is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure a medical condition.
