Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026
Last updated: September 18, 2026

QBINAURAL respects your privacy and is designed to minimize the collection and processing of personal data. The core functionality of the app operates locally on your device.

1. Personal Data We Collect

QBINAURAL does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or other identifying information.

QBINAURAL does not use third-party advertising networks, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics services to monitor users across apps or websites.

2. My Music

QBINAURAL includes a My Music feature that allows you to select compatible audio files available to you through Apple's system file picker.

Audio files selected using My Music are processed and played locally on your device. They are not uploaded to QBINAURAL servers, transmitted to the developer, or shared by QBINAURAL with third parties. The developer does not have access to the contents of audio files you select.

3. Audio Processing and App Settings

Binaural tone generation, audio processing, Pink and Brown Noise, timer functions, sound selections and related audio features are performed locally on your device.

QBINAURAL does not create a personal profile based on your audio selections or listening preferences and does not transmit those selections to the developer.

4. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

QBINAURAL does not contain third-party advertising networks and does not use third-party analytics or tracking services.

QBINAURAL does not track you across apps or websites for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

QBINAURAL does not intentionally transmit personal data or user-selected audio files to third parties.

Your download, purchase, installation, updates and certain operating-system functions may be handled by Apple. Any information processed independently by Apple through its services is subject to Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

QBINAURAL does not maintain a developer-controlled database containing users' personal data. Therefore, there is no personal user data stored on QBINAURAL servers for the developer to retain or delete.

Audio files and other information stored locally on your device remain under your control and the control of the operating system. Local app data can be removed by deleting applicable local files or uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

QBINAURAL does not knowingly collect personal data from children or other users. The app does not require user registration and does not maintain developer-controlled user profiles.

8. Security and Privacy by Design

QBINAURAL is designed according to a data-minimization approach. Core audio functionality is performed locally on your device, reducing the need to transmit personal information over the internet.

No software or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. QBINAURAL reduces privacy risks by avoiding unnecessary collection and transmission of personal data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if QBINAURAL's functionality, data practices, legal obligations, or App Store requirements change. If QBINAURAL's data practices materially change, this Privacy Policy and, where required, the relevant App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

QBINAURAL nie gromadzi, nie przesyła, nie sprzedaje, nie wynajmuje ani nie udostępnia danych osobowych użytkowników.

Aplikacja nie wymaga utworzenia konta ani podania imienia i nazwiska, adresu e-mail, numeru telefonu, lokalizacji ani innych danych umożliwiających identyfikację użytkownika.

QBINAURAL zawiera funkcję My Music, która umożliwia użytkownikowi wybranie zgodnych plików audio dostępnych za pośrednictwem systemowego selektora plików Apple.

Pliki audio wybrane za pomocą funkcji My Music są przetwarzane i odtwarzane lokalnie na urządzeniu użytkownika. 4. Analityka, reklamy i śledzenie

QBINAURAL nie zawiera zewnętrznych sieci reklamowych oraz nie korzysta z zewnętrznych usług analitycznych ani technologii śledzących.
